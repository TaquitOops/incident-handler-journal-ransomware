# Incident Handler's Journal – Entry 01

## Date:
December 31, 2025

## Entry:
1

## Description:
This journal entry documents a ransomware incident that impacted a small healthcare clinic after employees downloaded malicious attachments from phishing emails. The attack resulted in encrypted files and major operational disruption.

## Tool(s) Used:
- Incident Handler’s Journal
- Email analysis (conceptual)
- Malware and ransomware analysis (theoretical)

## The 5 W’s

### Who caused the incident?
The incident was caused by an organized group of unethical cybercriminals known for targeting healthcare organizations through ransomware attacks.

### What happened?
Employees received phishing emails containing malicious attachments. Once downloaded, ransomware was deployed and encrypted critical organizational files, including patient records.

### When did the incident occur?
The incident occurred on a Tuesday morning at approximately 9:00 a.m.

### Where did the incident happen?
The incident occurred within the internal network and computer systems of a small healthcare clinic in the United States.

### Why did the incident happen?
The incident occurred due to successful phishing attacks, lack of employee awareness, and insufficient email security controls that allowed malicious attachments to be executed.

## Additional Notes:
This incident highlights the importance of phishing awareness training, email filtering solutions, regular data backups, and an incident response plan to reduce the impact of ransomware attacks.
