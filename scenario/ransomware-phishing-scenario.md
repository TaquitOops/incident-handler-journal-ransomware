# Ransomware Incident Scenario

## Overview
A small U.S.-based healthcare clinic specializing in primary care services experienced a cybersecurity incident on a Tuesday morning at approximately 9:00 a.m.

## Incident Details
- Employees were unable to access medical records and critical systems.
- A ransom note appeared on affected computers stating files had been encrypted.
- Attackers demanded payment in exchange for the decryption key.
- The attackers are known for targeting healthcare and transportation sectors.

## Attack Vector
The attackers gained access through **targeted phishing emails** sent to multiple employees.  
Each email contained a **malicious attachment** that installed malware once downloaded.

## Impact
- Patient data became inaccessible
- Business operations were severely disrupted
- Systems were shut down
- External organizations were contacted for assistance

## Incident Type
- Phishing
- Malware infection
- Ransomware attack
